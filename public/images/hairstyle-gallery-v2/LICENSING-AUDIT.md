**Scope:** the 24 approved staged target images in `men/manifest.csv` and the `target_reference` records in `women/manifest.csv`. This is a local documentation and metadata audit; no external provider or paid API was contacted.

## Summary

| Status | Images |
|---|---:|
| VERIFIED | 0 |
| REQUIRES_PERMISSION | 0 |
| UNKNOWN | 24 |

No staged image has sufficient evidence to mark its provenance and commercial-use permission `VERIFIED`. No staged image has evidence identifying a rights holder from whom permission is specifically known to be required, so none is assigned `REQUIRES_PERMISSION` rather than assuming facts not in evidence. `UNKNOWN` means the file exists, but its creator/source chain and/or applicable commercial license could not be established from the available project records and file metadata.

`CONFIRMED` or `NEEDS_REVIEW` in the gallery manifests describes the visible hairstyle match only. Those labels do not establish copyright, authorship, model/property release, or commercial-use rights.

## Documentation and license evidence found

The project `README.md` documents ten separate Pexels photo references, names photographers, links individual Pexels pages, and links the [Pexels license](https://www.pexels.com/license/). It states that this license permits use on websites and apps and that attribution is not required (the project nevertheless records credits). This is project documentation about **different local files** in `public/images/hairstyles/`; it does not license or provide provenance for the 24 staged collage-derived files.

The README also says six earlier selectable candidate crops were from an AI-generated collage and that their licensing had not been verified. The `public/images/hairstyle-candidates/README.md` repeats that limitation. Those six candidate files are not treated here as the provenance chain for every v2 target, because the v2 manifests identify different collage files and the relationship is not established.

No license grants, creator statements, source URLs, attribution notices, model/property-release records, or documented commercial-use permissions were found for the four local source collages or their 24 staged target images. There are therefore **no source URLs for the 24 staged references** to report.

## Replacement recommendations

Until permission or an applicable license is documented, do not publish or integrate the 24 `UNKNOWN` references as commercial-use assets. First seek the original creator/source and written commercial-use permission for each collage and the Temple Fade source. If that cannot be established, replace affected references with individual images whose source page and license are recorded and whose visible hairstyle fits the target.

## Audit limits

- Status is assigned to each approved staged image, not to the separate Pexels alternatives.
- Local files and manifest-to-source mappings were inspected; no network browsing or external rights-holder contact was performed.
- `UNKNOWN` is not a finding that use is prohibited. It means available evidence is insufficient to confirm permission.
- Obtain appropriate rights evidence before using these images in a commercial demo, app, or public deployment.
